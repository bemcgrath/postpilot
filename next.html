What should we build

0.7 is out. This is the ballot for what comes next. Upvote the ideas you want — one click per idea, counts are live. Write-ins still welcome as a reply. Scheduling, auto-post, and X login are not on the list — those would break the product.

STILL LOCAL · STILL NO X LOGIN · YOU HIT SEND

How to vote

Tap ▲ on this page. You can upvote more than one idea, and tap again to undo. Optional: reply to @postpilotforx with a write-in or a ranked top 3 like 1, 6, 9.

On the ballot

1 Hook-only rewrite

Swap the first line, keep the body you already wrote. Three opener angles, same post.

2 Voice Match from your X archive

Drop the official tweet archive zip. Skip pasting five sample posts by hand.

3 Multiple local profiles

One Voice Match and insight set per X account on this machine. Switch when you switch accounts.

4 Quote-tweet rubric

Score a quote as add vs echo. Same honesty as reply-craft, for quotes.

5 Thread reorder

See the weakest tweet in a thread and move or rewrite it before you post. You still hit send.

6 Save a feed post to the Hook Library

One tap from Viral Post Analyzer. Steal the opener shape, not the words.

7 Score history calendar

A local heatmap of what you shipped, scores, and hook types — not another cloud dashboard.

8 Image and alt-text scoring

Judge the photo and the alt, not just “image attached +2.” Still on-device.

9 Firefox and Edge

Same extension, same local storage rules, not Chrome-only.

10 Keyboard shortcuts in compose

Expand the panel, save a draft, run a rewrite, without taking your hands off the post.

11 Custom governor packs

Import/export phrase lists you can share. Your slop list, your banned bait, not ours only.

12 Reply skip signal

When the parent isn’t worth a reply — the inverse of “invites a reply.” You still choose.

Not on the ballot

Schedulers. Auto-post. Auto-reply queues. Connecting your X account. Fake “14,200 impressions” forecasts. Scoring LinkedIn, Instagram, and twelve other networks. If you want those, a different tool already exists. PostPilot scores what you wrote, on your device, before you send.
