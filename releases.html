What's out

The compose box now scores media, names AI slop, and shows a reach range from your own posts — not a scheduler, not account access, not writing the post for you.

STILL LOCAL · STILL NO X LOGIN · YOU HIT SEND

Just shipped (v0.7.4) Aug 28, 2026

Shipped

Video scored separately from images

X's own published ranking algorithm (xai-org/x-algorithm) weights a video open differently from a photo expand — so now we do too, instead of folding video into the image score like every version before this one. Your video posts get their own cold-start default and, once you have enough of them, their own learned boost in Settings → Analytics. The image and link priors stay untouched — the public algorithm doesn't publish a link penalty, so we're not inventing one.

v0.7.2 Aug 24, 2026

Shipped

Weekly score trend chart

Settings → Analytics now plots your last 13 weeks of scores next to your 7-day average, so the two numbers always agree. It stays visible between posting weeks and tells you honestly when there's not enough recent history yet — built entirely from scores stored on your device.

v0.7.1 Aug 19, 2026

Shipped

New hook only

Swap the first line, keep the body you already wrote. Free gets one opener; Pro gets three angles. Replies stay a full rewrite — a reply isn’t a first-line-plus-body job.

Shipped · Pro

Voice Match shapes rewrite layout

Rewrites already used your vocabulary and person. They now match how you actually break lines — short lines, stanzas, fragments — even when this draft is a single block.

Shipped

Start here in Settings

Scoring works with no setup. Free: go write. Pro Voice Match: add five posts, then Analyze. Governor and hook knobs sit under Advanced. Leave them alone unless a score feels wrong.

v0.7 Aug 17, 2026

Shipped · Pro

Import your X Analytics CSV

Settings → Analytics. Download the tweet-level export X already gives Premium users (Tweet id / Tweet text — not the by-day summary). Drop it in. Insights fill in without waiting for 20 scraped posts. Profile scraping still runs in the background.

Shipped

Images and links move the score

Attach a photo or paste a link and the badge updates. Same as the text — scored on your device, using what actually worked on your account when we have the data.

Shipped

“Is now a good time?” + a pre-publish checklist

A last look before send: hook, flagged phrases, length, media. Warns. Never blocks.

Shipped

AI-slop warnings

When a draft sounds like ChatGPT — bland templates, telltale phrasing — PostPilot names it. The algorithm punishes robotic copy. So do we.

Shipped · Pro

A reach range from your posts

Not a made-up “14,200 people.” A range based on how similar posts of yours actually did, with the sample size shown. Hidden until there’s enough data to be honest.

Shipped

Compare two of your drafts

Short vs long. Question vs claim. See which one scores higher and why, side by side.

Shipped

Does this post invite a reply?

Stopping the scroll and starting a conversation are different jobs. You’ll see if the draft is a broadcast or a prompt — without rewarding “thoughts?” bait.

Shipped · Optional

A self-reply prompt after you post

One suggestion for a follow-up tweet. You still paste and send it. PostPilot never posts for you.
