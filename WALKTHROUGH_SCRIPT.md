# PostPilot walkthrough — recording script

A shot list for the "how to actually get value out of PostPilot" video requested
in user feedback (2026-09-03), distinct from the extension-install walkthrough
already made during the thread contest. That one covers *getting PostPilot on
your machine*; this one covers *getting PostPilot to understand your writing*.

Target: ~4–5 minutes, screen recording + voiceover. Each scene has the on-screen
action and talking points — not a word-for-word script, since it should sound
like whoever records it, not like a script being read. Say it in your own
words; the point of each beat is what matters.

Flow, per the feedback: **Voice Profile → niche → write/paste a post → analyze
it → understand the score → refine it → post.**

---

## Scene 1 — Cold open (0:00–0:20)

**On screen:** X compose box, empty. Cursor blinking.

**Talking points:** One sentence on the problem — you don't know if a post is
any good until you've already sent it and watched it flop. PostPilot scores it
*before* you hit send, right in the compose box, and it's local — no login, no
account access.

---

## Scene 2 — Open Settings, land on Voice (0:20–0:45)

**On screen:** Click the PostPilot toolbar icon → popup → "Voice Match" link.
Settings opens on a new tab. Click the **Voice** tab.

**Talking points:** "Before we look at scoring, let's set up Voice Match — this
is the part that took me longest to understand, so I want to show it first."
This is the single most important framing line in the whole video — it directly
answers "what am I supposed to do first."

---

## Scene 3 — What the Voice Profile box actually is (0:45–1:30)

**On screen:** The Voice tab: the hint text above the box ("Voice Match already
works from 5+ posts on the Posts tab...") and the four-item guide list (Niche,
Hook Preferences, Length, Tone).

**Talking points:**
- This box is optional, not a form you have to fill out — 5 pasted posts on
  the Posts tab is already enough for most people.
- This box is for people who want to *hand-tune* what Voice Match knows about
  you: your niche, which hook types you lean on, your target length, your tone.
- Read the four guide bullets out loud, briefly, in your own words.

---

## Scene 4 — Load the example, walk through it (1:30–2:30)

**On screen:** Click **Load Example**. The box fills with the worked template.
Scroll through it slowly: `## Niche`, `## Hook Preferences` table, `## Length`,
`## Tone`.

**Talking points, one per section:**
- **Niche** — "This is just a list of keywords that describe what you post
  about. PostPilot uses this to keep AI rewrites on-topic for you."
- **Hook Preferences** — "This is a ranked table of the opener styles that
  actually work for you. If you're not sure, look at your best-performing
  posts on the Posts tab first, then rank whatever pattern shows up."
- **Length** — "Your typical post length. This sets the 'sweet spot' range
  the live score checks your drafts against."
- **Tone** — "Plain-language notes on how you actually write — first person
  or not, short punchy lines or longer ones, that kind of thing."

Then: "Now I'll edit this to actually be about *my* niche" — swap a couple of
the example's keywords/hook ranking for real ones, live, so viewers see it's a
document you edit, not a form you're stuck with.

---

## Scene 5 — Add sample posts (2:30–3:00)

**On screen:** Switch to the **Posts** tab. Paste a few real past posts (or
show the "Import From Your Best Posts" list if there are collected candidates).
Click through to at least 5.

**Talking points:** "Posts fill in the stuff a profile document can't —
sentence length, how often I use fragments, that kind of statistical pattern.
Five is the minimum, more is better."

---

## Scene 6 — Generate the fingerprint (3:00–3:15)

**On screen:** Click **Save Profile** / generate. Cut to x.com — the compose
bar now shows a **Voice Match** badge.

**Talking points:** One line — "That's it, Voice Match is live now."

---

## Scene 7 — Write a post, watch the score (3:15–3:50)

**On screen:** Type a real post from scratch in the X compose box. The
PostPilot score panel updates live underneath.

**Talking points:** Narrate the score panel as it moves — hook type detected,
character count against the sweet spot, any governor warning that pops up,
the Voice Match delta. "This number is live — watch it change as I edit this
line."

---

## Scene 8 — Understand the breakdown (3:50–4:15)

**On screen:** Click to expand the panel. Point at the governor issues list
and the hook-type explanation.

**Talking points:** Explain what a governor warning actually means (a phrase
pattern that tends to suppress engagement — not a hard block, just a flag) and
what the hook type is telling you.

---

## Scene 9 — AI rewrite, refine (4:15–4:45)

**On screen:** Click the AI rewrite button. Show the suggestion appear with
its own (higher) score next to the original.

**Talking points:** This is the beat to land the thing you told us you liked —
"it doesn't just rewrite the whole thing, it keeps what I already had and
makes it better." Show yourself accepting part of it and still tweaking a word
or two by hand.

---

## Scene 10 — Post it (4:45–5:00)

**On screen:** Click X's own post button. PostPilot never touches this step.

**Talking points:** Closing line — PostPilot never posts for you, it just
makes sure what you're about to post is worth sending. Point to
`manual.html` for anyone who wants the full reference.

---

## Notes for whoever records this

- Use a real account with real posts, not placeholder text — the "this
  actually understood my writing" moment only lands if the example is real.
- Scene 3–4 is the part that matters most, per the feedback that prompted
  this script. Don't rush it even if the rest gets trimmed for time.
- If this gets cut down further, Scenes 3, 4, 7, and 9 are the ones to keep —
  they're the direct answers to "what goes in a voice profile" and "does the
  rewrite actually sound like me."
